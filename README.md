# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Data Analysis Using Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an E-Commerce Sales dataset containing 2,000 transactions recorded between 2023 and 2025. The objective is to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing behaviour, and generate actionable insights using Microsoft Excel.

The project uses data cleaning, PivotTables, descriptive analysis, and interactive visualizations to transform raw sales data into meaningful business information.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing regions and product categories.
- Evaluate monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Analyze customer loyalty levels by gender.
- Compare sales performance across different store types.
- Provide recommendations for business improvement.

## 📊 Key Analysis and Findings

### 1. Regional and Category Performance

- North is the highest-performing region, contributing approximately 31% of total sales.
- West records comparatively lower sales performance.
- Sports and Electronics are the leading product categories.
- Clothing shows relatively lower demand.

### 2. Monthly and Quarterly Sales Trends

- November 2024 recorded the highest monthly revenue.
- Seasonal sales peaks were observed during Q4 and March–April.
- Moderate discounts of approximately 14–16% were associated with stronger revenue periods.
- 2025 sales performance requires further monitoring because of incomplete data.

### 3. Payment Method Analysis

| Payment Method | Contribution |
|---|---:|
| PayPal | 35% |
| COD | 33% |
| Credit Card | 32% |

Payment distribution is relatively balanced across all three methods.

### 4. Customer Loyalty Analysis

- Female customers show stronger representation in Gold and Platinum segments.
- Male customers show relatively higher participation in Bronze.
- Silver customers represent an opportunity for loyalty upgrades.
- Platinum customers are an important retention segment.

### 5. Sales Channel Analysis

| Channel | Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Online | 41% |
| Flagship | 40% |
| Outlet | 19% |

Online and Flagship channels contribute nearly equal shares, while Outlet has comparatively lower contribution.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase marketing activities in the West region.
- Expand inventory for Sports and Electronics.
- Introduce seasonal promotions for Clothing.
- Prepare inventory before peak sales periods.
- Encourage digital payments through cashback offers.
- Develop loyalty upgrade campaigns for Bronze and Silver customers.
- Improve Outlet store performance through pricing and assortment strategies.
- Strengthen Online marketing and delivery operations.

## ⚠️ Project Limitations

- October 2025 contains partial data.
- Customer age and income information is unavailable.
- Profit margin and operating cost details are not included.
- The analysis primarily focuses on revenue and transaction behaviour.

## 🏁 Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of Microsoft Excel for transforming raw E-Commerce sales data into meaningful business insights.

The analysis highlights regional performance, product demand, seasonal sales patterns, payment preferences, customer loyalty, and sales channel contribution.

The findings can support business decisions related to inventory planning, promotional strategies, customer retention, and sales channel optimization.
